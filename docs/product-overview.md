# Product Overview

English | [简体中文](product-overview.zh.md)

## What it is

Toby is your personal study companion: it keeps your schedule, your todos, your money, and your notes in order — the things worth remembering in your life.

It runs on your own machine, for you alone. No accounts, no cloud, no data leaving your computer.

## How you use it

Open the page and say one plain sentence:

> "Remind me to pay the bill at noon tomorrow"
> "I need to clean my shoes this week"
> "Spent 32 on the taxi and 18 on lunch today"

Toby never touches your data directly. Instead it hands you a **proposal card**: what to record, where, and with which values — in plain sight.

Worried the AI might act on its own? It cannot: drafting is all it can do. The pen stays in your hand.

## Where you use it

- **Computer** (`/`): for organizing and batch work
- **Phone** (`/m/`): say a sentence from anywhere

## What it keeps track of

- **Schedule** — the things to watch each day; ask and it tells you
- **Todos** — one sentence onto the board; check them off when done
- **Money** — several entries in one sentence, with monthly totals
- **Notes** — your original words, as a monthly journal or topic notebooks
- **Library** — import files; browse booklists and chapters anytime
- Plus **greeting** and **memory**: it remembers your preferences and habits, and gets handier the longer you use it

The foundation comes first; these abilities land one by one.

## What it promises you

- Your data lives only on your own machine
- The AI can only draft proposals: never write on its own, never read your files, never run commands
- You see the full change before anything is written; cancel always means zero writes
- If a write fails midway, everything rolls back — no half-written state
